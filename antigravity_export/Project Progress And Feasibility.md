### Is It Feasible?

**Yes, the project is completely feasible.** Here is why the architecture works, along with the specific hurdles to watch out for:

#### 1. Why the architecture is sound
- **Constrained Ontology**: Instead of an unconstrained "open extraction" that leads to noisy graphs and edge explosion, the extractor restricts nodes to 5 types and relations to 4 types (`DEVELOPED`, `EVALUATED_ON`, `IMPROVES`, `CONTRADICTS`). This makes LLM extraction remarkably consistent.
- **Coherent Research Subfield**: The 16 papers chosen (Attention, BERT, RoBERTa, GPT-3, FlashAttention) form a clear, direct evolutionary lineage. They will naturally produce dense interconnected subgraphs.
- **Direct Neo4j + Force Graph Visualization**: Rather than just returning raw text, the backend passes intermediate Cypher steps to `react-force-graph-2d`, allowing users to inspect the factual subgraphs that justified the answer.

#### 2. Key practical challenges & how they are handled
- **Gemini Free Tier RPM / Quota**:
  - Ingesting all 16 papers (~350 chunks) at 15 RPM takes roughly 25 minutes.
  - *Mitigation*: [extractor.py](file:///home/rohan/Documents/CodingProjects/GraphRAG/ingestion/extractor.py) already has exponential backoff and pacing delays. We also have [ingest_corpus.py](file:///home/rohan/Documents/CodingProjects/GraphRAG/ingestion/ingest_corpus.py) which can ingest paper-by-paper and resume without re-processing.
- **Entity ID Discrepancies**:
  - If one paper extracts `"Vaswani et al."` and another `"Vaswani"`, or `"Multi-Head Attention"` vs `"Attention"`, links could miss.
  - *Mitigation*: Basic normalization exists in `extractor.py`, and queries use `toLower()` and `CONTAINS` filters in Cypher.
- **Text-to-Cypher Syntax Errors**:
  - LLMs occasionally invert arrow directions (e.g. `(BERT)-[:IMPROVES]->(RoBERTa)` instead of `(RoBERTa)-[:IMPROVES]->(BERT)`).
  - *Mitigation*: The few-shot prompt in `cypher_chain.py` includes explicit directional examples and a retry loop. Additionally, the headline feature queries are pre-written Cypher and bypass LLM generation entirely.
